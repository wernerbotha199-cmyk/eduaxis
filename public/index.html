WELCOME TO EDUAXIS

A little support can open up big possibilities.

EduAxis Tutor Centre is a small, welcoming learning environment in Villieria, Pretoria. We believe learners do their best when they feel understood, supported and encouraged to take the next step.

Our focus is on individual attention, steady academic development and the confidence to become a more independent learner. We work alongside parents and families to support each learner’s educational journey.

GRADES & CURRICULUM

Support for the next step.

EduAxis supports learners from Grade 1 through Grade 12 with academic tutoring and learning support aligned to the learner’s curriculum and programme arrangements.

Important: Parents register their children for home education. EduAxis provides tutoring, academic support and supervision of the learning programme; it is not advertised as a registered independent school.

FOR PARENTS & GUARDIANS

A learning partnership, built together.

Parents and guardians remain central to each learner’s home-education journey. EduAxis works with families to understand learning goals, agree on support arrangements and communicate progress.

Before enrolment, we encourage families to discuss the learner’s current grade, curriculum arrangements, academic needs, preferred schedule and any practical support requirements.

Home education: Parents are responsible for registering their child for home education where required. EduAxis provides tutoring, academic support and supervision of the agreed learning programme.
